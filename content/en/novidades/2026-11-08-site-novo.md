---
title: "O Carro Elétrico has a new look"
date: "2026-11-08"
---

The site has been rewritten from scratch. Each topic gives you the essentials first and shows the law
and the sources behind it. And it's now in English too.

- **More topics**: charging at home and in your building, on the road, batteries, grants and tax, and pages for professionals.
- **Sources in plain sight**: every page ends with the legislation, standards and bodies it relies on.
- **Grants always up to date**: they open and close on the page by themselves, following the dates in the official notice.

Prices stay where they change every day: on [ohmigo](https://ohmigo.ocarroeletrico.com/) and the
[CEME comparison](https://ceme.ocarroeletrico.com/). The 2023 charger tables are gone: they were out
of date. The content comes from Voltaria, an open project that gathers the rules of electric mobility
in Portugal, every fact with its source.
