title: "Why a fast charger gives less than its nameplate: we explain"
date: "2026-11-22"
---

The power on a fast charger's nameplate is the charger's maximum, not what your car gets. The
charging technology page now explains why.

- **Voltage and current**: a 500 A cable on a 400 V car gives at most about 200 kW.
- **800 V cars**: at a 500 V charger they charge more slowly, and without a converter not at all.
- **Shared power**: with two cars on the same charger, each gets less.

All of it in [why a fast charger gives you less than its
nameplate](/tecnologia-de-carregamento/#potencia-em-dc).
