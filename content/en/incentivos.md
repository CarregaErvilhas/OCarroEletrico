It depends on the day. The money for buying an electric car or a charging point comes mostly from the
Fundo Ambiental, the public environmental fund, through calls that open and close, with a deadline
and a fixed budget. What's open today comes right below, with the amounts from the official notice.
And some things don't depend on calls at all: a fully electric car pays neither IUC nor ISV and, in
some towns, parks for free or at a discount.

:::essencial
- **Fundo Ambiental**: it funds the purchase of new vehicles and of charging points through calls with a fixed budget, and you apply online only, until the deadline or until the money runs out.
- **Announced isn't open**: the Social Climate Plan foresees support for vulnerable households and micro-enterprises, but without a call there are no rules, amounts or dates.
- **Taxes**: a fully electric car pays neither IUC nor ISV and, at a company, up to the ordinance's limit, it deducts the purchase VAT and escapes autonomous taxation.
:::

### Before you apply, answer six questions {#antes-de-te-candidatares}

These answers tell you whether a grant is for you, and which one.

1. **Individual or company?** Or a charity (IPSS), or a local authority. The rules and amounts change.
2. **Which vehicle?** Car, motorbike, bicycle, e-scooter, or a charging point.
3. **New or used?** The Fundo Ambiental's purchase grants require a new vehicle.
4. **Have you bought it already or are you about to, and on what date?** Expenses only count from a date set in the notice.
5. **For a car: what's the final price including VAT, and do you have a combustion car to scrap (abate)?** There are price limits and scrapping requirements.
6. **For a charging point: house or condominium?** The notice says whether the grant covers houses. In a building, what the law lets you install is in [charging at home](/carregar-em-casa/#num-predio).

### How applying works {#como-funciona-a-candidatura}

- **Online only**, on the Fundo Ambiental website. They don't accept documents by email.
- **First come, first served.** Applications are ranked by date and time of submission. They close
  on the deadline or when the budget runs out, whichever comes first.
- **The grant comes with obligations.** You have to keep the vehicle or the charging point for a
  minimum period, and you can't export the vehicle.
- **The amount that counts is the notice's.** A figure you read at a dealership, from a car brand or
  on a blog isn't a source. The sources are the Fundo Ambiental, the Tax Authority (Autoridade
  Tributária) and the official gazette (Diário da República).

:::aviso
**Approved isn't paid yet.** After approval there's a deadline to accept and request payment, with the invoices in the name of whoever applied. If you applied to an edition that has already closed, the deadline is in its notice, in the [history](/incentivos/#historico) further down.
:::

## Announced isn't open: the Social Climate Plan {#anunciado-nao-e-aberto}

An announced grant doesn't exist yet for applications. The rules only apply once the call or the law
is published. Until then, it's an announcement.

On the table is the Social Climate Plan (Plano Social para o Clima) 2026-2032, which the Government
submitted to the European Commission. It foresees support for vulnerable households, to electrify
their mobility, and for micro-enterprises, to electrify their fleets. It still depends on the

### What you read in the papers isn't a rule {#o-que-se-leu-nos-jornais}

In the debate on the 2027 State Budget (Orçamento do Estado), on 28 October 2026, the Minister for the
Environment and Energy spoke of covering up to 80% of the car's value. She also spoke of used cars up to
five years old and of priority for people on the social electricity tariff. The papers added payment by
voucher, an obligation to keep the car and maximum amounts that don't match each other.

None of this is in a call or a law. They are statements and estimates, not rules, and they may change
before the call comes out.

## Taxes: what an electric car doesn't pay {#impostos}

Here there are no calls and no application deadlines. These are rules in the tax codes, applied by the
Tax Authority.

- **IUC**, the annual circulation tax: fully electric vehicles are exempt.
- **ISV**, the vehicle tax: fully electric vehicles are outside it. It matters, for example, when you
  import a used electric car.
- **VAT (IVA)**, for companies and self-employed people entitled to deduct it: VAT on buying or
  leasing electric or plug-in hybrid cars is deductible up to an acquisition cost set by ordinance
  (portaria). So is the VAT on the electricity used in them.
- **Autonomous taxation (tributação autónoma)**, under corporate tax (IRC): costs of fully electric
  cars only pay it if the acquisition cost goes over the ordinance's limit.

## Parking: free in some towns {#estacionamento}

Some town halls (câmaras) exempt electric cars from paying for parking, or give a discount. The
conditions change from town to town: whether you need a municipal sticker (dístico) and what it costs,
whether it only covers fully electric cars or only passenger cars, whether it only applies in certain
car parks.

The table comes from the list compiled by the UVE, the Portuguese EV users' association, not from the
town halls. Each row has a link for you to check. Before you park without paying, check with the town hall.

{{factos kind=municipal}}

- **The list isn't exhaustive.** If your town isn't in the table, that doesn't mean there's no
  benefit. Look at the website of the town hall or of the municipal parking company.
- **Restricted-access zones**: according to the UVE, some towns have zones like these from which fully
  electric cars are exempt. They aren't in this table: look at the town hall's website.

### The IMT sticker is no longer mandatory {#distico-do-imt}

Lei n.º 19/2024, of 5 February, ended the obligation to have the electric vehicle sticker and took
away the IMT's (the transport authority's) power to issue it. Don't ask the IMT for one. The sticker
came from the previous electric mobility regime, which is no longer in force.

Stickers already issued remain valid. According to the UVE, some municipalities still ask for it for
the parking exemption, and others require their own municipal sticker. The table above says which.

## History: what the Fundo Ambiental gave in each edition {#historico}

Grants that have already closed stay here, one per edition, newest first. They no longer accept
applications. They're useful for three things:

- **Comparing years**: how much the grant was in an edition, and whether it has gone up or down.
  Always compare the same type, a car for an individual with a car for an individual.
- **Seeing how the rules changed**: for example, when scrapping became a requirement or when the price
  limit changed.
- **Knowing which rules apply to you**, if you applied to one of these editions.

Past editions' dates show when the next one usually opens, but they don't promise it. The rules of
each edition are those of its notice or order (despacho).

## Myths and mistakes {#mitos-e-enganos}

- **"There are no grants for electric cars."** An empty list of open grants only means we know of none
  open on this date. The Fundo Ambiental opens calls yearly or in phases: keep an eye on its website.
- **"The State will pay up to 80% of the car, even a used one."** That's what the Minister for the
  Environment and Energy said in the 2027 State Budget debate. It isn't in any call or law: it's a
  statement, and it may change.
- **"Better to wait for the new grant."** There's no opening date and no guarantee your case fits. If
  you need a car now, decide with what exists today.
- **"The Fundo Ambiental also funds used cars."** The Fundo Ambiental's purchase grants require a new
  vehicle.
- **"I have to ask the IMT for the sticker."** Not any more. Lei n.º 19/2024 ended the obligation, and
  the IMT stopped issuing it.
- **"My town isn't on the list, so I pay."** Not necessarily. The UVE's list isn't exhaustive: check
  on the town hall's website.
